About Me

Hi, I'm Alessandra

I have had a strong passion for art and videogames since I was a child. I am an energetic person with a great desire to acquire new skills, loving to draw and bring imaginary things to life for games.

With a degree in scenography, I am deeply passionate about creating imaginary environments, placing objects within spaces to tell a compelling story.

Software & Skills: Maya, ZBrush, Unreal Engine 5, Substance Painter, Photoshop, 2D Concept Art, Hard Surface & Organic Sculpting, Retopology & UV Unwrapping.
